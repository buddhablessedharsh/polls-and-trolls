2. Open the meme_archive_website folder.
3. Double-click index.html to preview the site in your browser.
4. Keep styles.css, script.js, archive-data.js, and the assets folder together.

METADATA
The original spreadsheet records are preserved in archive-data.js. The individual case-study pages use the metadata as supporting documentation, shown in an expandable “View archival metadata” panel. The main page content is explanatory text, meme images, and research links.

IMAGE USE
Images copied from the supplied metadata workbook are displayed on the pages where the current metadata suggests they are relevant. Topic-to-image placement is provisional and should be checked against the source URLs and original image context before publication. No new meme images were invented.

RESEARCH AND INTERPRETATION
- Some source URLs may be unavailable, moved, or require an account.
- Dates in the spreadsheet may be observation dates, not original creation dates.
- Metadata labels and transcribed captions are archival descriptions, not independent verification of claims in a meme.
- The Russian-interference page distinguishes documented account tactics from evidence about audience effects.
- The Pepe page follows the supplied document and preserves the distinction between hateful variants and non-bigoted uses.
- This is a small, evolving sample, not a representative census of election memes.

The design and copy are a working draft. Update page content, source citations, and image placement after reviewing the user's sketch and confirming image-to-topic mapping.
